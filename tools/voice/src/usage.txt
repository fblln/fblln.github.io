voice — check an article draft against the voice of the published corpus.

  voice <draft.md>                check against the stored bands; exit 1 on drift
  voice <draft.md> --recalibrate  check against the corpus as it is right now
  voice --calibrate               show the bands and every published article
  voice --calibrate --write       regenerate tools/voice/bands.txt
  --corpus <dir>                  default content/articles

The bands live in tools/voice/bands.txt, compiled in, with every article's
numbers beside them. They are stored rather than recomputed because a gate
that recalibrates itself cannot fail: one article with long paragraphs raises
the ceiling for the next one, and the voice ratchets with nothing going red.
